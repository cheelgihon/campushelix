🗺️ Your Roadmap

From "I want to" to "I'm in."

A realistic 18-month journey — most students underestimate how early it really starts. Here's the path that actually works.

1
12–18 months out

Decide & shortlist 🎯

Pick countries and majors that fit your goals. Shortlist 8–12 universities.

2
8–14 months out

Take standardized tests 📝

TOEFL/IELTS, SAT/ACT for US, HSK for China. Plan 1–2 retakes — most students need them.

3
Ongoing — start now

Build your profile 🌱

High GPA, leadership in extracurriculars, olympiad/competition wins. Depth beats breadth.

4
6–10 months out

Apply for scholarships 💰

Major scholarships open 6–18 months early. MEXT, GKS, CGS, Stipendium — track every deadline.

5
2–6 months out

Essays & applications ✍️

Personal statement (5–10 drafts), rec letters, Common App / UCAS submissions. Track every deadline.

6
1–3 months before

Visa & departure ✈️

Visa application, financial proof, accommodation, flights. Pure logistics once acceptance lands.

⏳ Reality Check

Why you have to prepare early.

Most students underestimate how much groundwork study abroad takes. Here's what the data and the timeline actually look like.

📅

Standardized tests take months

TOEFL, IELTS, SAT, ACT typically need 3–6 months of consistent practice to hit competitive scores.

🎯

Top universities are very selective

Ivy League, MIT, Stanford accept under 10% of applicants. Many require near-perfect test scores.

⏰

Scholarships close very early

Major scholarships (Chevening, DAAD, MEXT) close 6–12 months before the academic year starts.

✍️

Essays require many drafts

A strong personal statement typically goes through 5–10 revisions. You cannot do this in a weekend.
